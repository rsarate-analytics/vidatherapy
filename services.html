Our services

Support that meets your needs.

Vida offers compassionate, practical support for emotional wellbeing, family relationships, trauma recovery, and meaningful growth across life and work.

01

Anxiety & Panic Support

Individual therapy for anxiety, panic attacks, overwhelm, emotional exhaustion, and practical coping strategies that bring steadiness and focus.

02

Trauma & PTSD Recovery

Compassionate support for trauma, PTSD, intrusive thoughts, hypervigilance, and rebuilding a sense of safety, control, and resilience.

03

Family & Relationship Counselling

Guidance for couples, parents, and families facing conflict, communication challenges, disconnection, and relationship strain.

04

Domestic Violence Support

Safe, supportive counselling for individuals impacted by domestic violence, coercive control, fear, and the need for healing and empowerment.

05

Bilingual Therapy

English and Spanish counselling for Hispanic and Latino adults, teens, families, and couples navigating stress, identity, acculturation, and emotional health.

06

Vocational & Life Direction

Support for burnout, career transitions, life purpose, and practical planning to help you move forward with clarity and confidence.
