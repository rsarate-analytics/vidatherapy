Who I work with

Support that feels safe, empowering, and human.

Ayleen works with individuals navigating anxiety, stress, relationship challenges, self-esteem, grief, and major life transitions. Her approach is warm, collaborative, and tailored to each person’s pace and goals.

Whether someone is feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or ready for change, Ayleen offers a supportive, grounded space to explore what is happening and what support may be needed next.

Practical insight
