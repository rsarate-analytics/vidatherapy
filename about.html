Who I work with

Support that feels safe, empowering, and human.

Ayleen supports adults, teens, couples, and families navigating anxiety, panic attacks, trauma, family conflict, relationship strain, burnout, parenting stress, addiction challenges, and cultural adjustment across the Greater Toronto Area.

With more than 25 years of experience in vocational counselling and child protection, she brings a practical, compassionate, and culturally informed approach to care. Her bilingual counselling in English and Spanish helps Hispanic and Latino clients feel understood, supported, and equipped for meaningful change.

She works with clients in North York, Etobicoke, Mississauga, Brampton, and surrounding communities, offering integrative support grounded in CBT, Person-Centered Therapy, ACT, Gestalt Therapy, Solution-Focused Brief Therapy, and family systems work.
